👋 Quick App Guide

How to Use This App

📚 BIKAT Education
🛍️ Wolkite Shop
1
Select Your Stream

Tap Remedial or Freshman (Natural / Social). You can change it anytime with the orange 🔄 Change Stream button.

2
Choose Subject → Questions or Exams

Tap any subject, then pick 📚 Questions (chapter practices) or 📝 Exams (Mid & Final past exams).

3
Earn Points & Unlock Chapters / VIP

Reach 50 pts (15 days), 150 pts (1 month), or 300 pts (1 month) to unlock the next chapter! Invite 5 friends or pay 50 ETB for 1 full subject (1 month), or 150 ETB for Full Semester VIP.

1
Browse or Search Items

Filter by category or search for clothes, electronics, books, and dorm items on campus.

2
Order, Call, or Chat

Tap any item to see photos, reviews, and tap 🛒 Order Now, 📞 Call, or 💬 Chat directly with the seller.

3
Open Your Own Shop

Go to My Shop to register your shop, then tap the center + Sell button to upload products.
